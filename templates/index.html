What decides the outcome

Age

Lenders read age as repayment horizon — how long you'll likely stay banked with them.

Salary

Your income sets the ceiling on the credit limit a lender is willing to offer.

Employment type

Salaried, self-employed, or business — each carries a different risk weighting.

CIBIL score

Your repayment history on record, out of 900. The single most-weighted input.

City tier

Tier 1, 2, or 3 cities carry different cost-of-living and default-rate assumptions.

FOIR score

Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio — how much of your salary is already spoken for.
